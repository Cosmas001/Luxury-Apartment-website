The Residence

A home composed
in light and sky.

Offered at $38,500,000

Occupying the entire fifty-eighth floor of The Aurelian, Penthouse 58 is a rare composition of space and serenity: a sanctuary suspended above the skyline.

Designed in collaboration with an award-winning atelier, the residence unfolds across 7,400 square feet of interior living and 2,100 square feet of wraparound terraces. Honed Calacatta marble, rift-sawn white oak and hand-finished bronze details are set against floor-to-ceiling glass, so that every room is framed by an ever-changing panorama.

A private elevator opens directly into a gallery foyer. From there, the grand salon, formal dining room and chef's kitchen flow effortlessly toward the terraces. The principal suite spans the eastern wing and greets each morning with sunrise over the water.
